A gentle reminder

Forgetting a name and remembering it later is common at any age. Getting lost in familiar places, repeating the same questions, or struggling with everyday tasks is not a normal part of ageing — those are reasons to see a doctor. Many causes of memory trouble (low vitamin B12, thyroid problems, medicine side-effects, depression, poor sleep, hearing loss) are treatable.[2]
