🎮 Memory games

Six games that exercise different kinds of memory. Pick a level, turn the sound on 🔊, and play a few rounds each day. Your best scores are saved on this device.

Games are a fun way to stay mentally active. Research suggests the benefits of brain training are mostly for the skills you practise, so mix games with exercise, social time and learning new things.

🃏 Memory Match visual memory

Turn over two cards at a time and find all the matching pairs in as few moves as you can.
