What to note

  • Memory: what you forgot, how often, and whether it's new. Repeating questions, missed appointments or bills, getting lost on familiar routes.
  • Mood: low mood, worry, losing interest in things — depression and anxiety can affect memory.
  • Sleep: hours, snoring, waking at night, daytime sleepiness.
  • Medications: new or changed medicines (including over-the-counter sleep aids or allergy pills), missed doses.
  • Other: hearing or vision changes, falls, illness, big life events.

Game and exercise scores are only a rough personal record — they are not clinical test results. See memory tests doctors use.
